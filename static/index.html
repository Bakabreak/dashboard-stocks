How is the sentiment judged?
  • Word matching, not AI. Each headline and summary is scanned for positive words (beats, surges, upgrade, record, raises guidance…) and negative words (misses, plunges, downgrade, lawsuit, layoffs, warns…).
  • Score: words in the headline count double; positive score → Positive, negative → Negative, otherwise Neutral.
  • Relevance: articles that don't mention the company or ticker are tagged Market-wide, dimmed, and left out of the summary.
  • Summary: counts the company-specific articles from the last 3 days.
  • Limits: it misses sarcasm, negation ("not a miss") and context. Read the headlines and treat the label as a hint only.
